Custom-Built Metal Products

Every product we make is built to order, using premium materials and finished to the highest standards. Browse our catalogue and contact us for a custom quote.

Main Entrance Gates

Heavy-duty decorative main gates crafted in mild steel and wrought iron. Available in sliding, swing, and bi-fold designs with optional automation.

Designer Window Grills

Elegant and secure window grills in modern, floral, and geometric patterns. Custom sizes available to fit any window dimension.

Balcony Grills & Railings

Stylish balcony and terrace railings that blend safety with aesthetics. Available in powder-coated and stainless steel finishes.

Spiral Staircases

Stunning spiral staircases engineered for safety and visual impact. Ideal for compact spaces, available in multiple tread and railing styles.

Durable steel sheds, car parkings, and canopies built to withstand weather. Custom sizes for residential, commercial, and industrial use.

Roof Trusses & Frames
