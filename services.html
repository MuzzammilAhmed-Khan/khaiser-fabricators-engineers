Comprehensive Fabrication & Engineering Services

We offer complete, end-to-end solutions — from concept design and engineering analysis to manufacturing and on-site erection — ensuring precision, durability, and performance in every project.

Design & Engineering

Customized solutions based on functional and structural requirements. Our engineering team provides technical drawings, load calculations, and end-to-end design support to ensure every project is built right from the start.

Technical Drawings Structural Design Engineering Analysis

Fabrication

Precision metal fabrication with strict quality control. We handle the complete fabrication cycle — from material selection and cutting to welding and finishing — for both small-scale components and large structural assemblies.

Metal Cutting Welding Quality Control Finishing

Load & Stress Analysis

Ensuring safety, durability, and optimized performance through rigorous structural analysis. We evaluate load-bearing capacity, stress distribution, and fatigue life to guarantee that every fabricated structure meets safety standards.

Load Analysis Stress Testing Safety Compliance

Access Control Gates

Secure and customized entry systems designed for residential, commercial, and industrial premises. We fabricate robust access gates tailored to client specifications, combining strength with aesthetic appeal.

Security Gates Custom Design Residential & Commercial

Automatic Gates

Design, supply, and installation of automated gate solutions. We work with industry-leading cantilever gate hardware — including Genoamatic systems (Giovanni-1200, Moderato-800, Mino-400) — to deliver smooth, reliable, and durable automated entry systems.

Cantilever Gates Gate Automation Supply & Installation

Spare Parts Manufacturing

Custom-built components manufactured with high accuracy. We produce precision spare parts for industrial machinery and equipment, ensuring compatibility, longevity, and minimal downtime for your operations.

Custom Components High Precision Industrial Parts

Metal Structures & Frames

Structural assemblies, supports, and frameworks fabricated to precise engineering standards. From mezzanine floors and support frames to industrial racks and platforms, we deliver structures built for load and longevity.

Structural Assemblies Support Frames Industrial Platforms

Erection Projects

On-site installation and structural erection works carried out by our experienced field team. We manage the complete erection process — from site preparation and alignment to final commissioning — ensuring safety and precision at every stage.

On-Site Installation Structural Erection Commissioning

Process Equipment & Specialized Fabrication

We design and fabricate specialized process equipment for industrial applications, built to exact specifications with high-grade materials.

10,000 Litre Aluminium Tanks

Large-capacity aluminium storage tanks fabricated for industrial and chemical applications, engineered for strength and corrosion resistance.

Stainless Steel (SS) Tanks

Food-grade and industrial-grade stainless steel tanks built to hygienic standards, suitable for storage, processing, and transport applications.

Mixing Tanks

Custom mixing tanks for industrial applications, designed with appropriate agitator mounts, baffles, and discharge configurations to meet process requirements.

Chemical Storage & Process Tanks

Heavy-duty tanks fabricated for chemical storage and process operations, with material selection and design optimized for chemical compatibility and safety.

SPM – Special Purpose Machines

Custom-built machines designed for specific operational needs. Our SPMs are engineered from scratch to meet unique production requirements where standard equipment falls short.
