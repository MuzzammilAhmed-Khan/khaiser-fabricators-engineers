Comprehensive Fabrication & Engineering Services

We offer end-to-end metal fabrication solutions, from design and cutting to welding, finishing, and installation. Each service is delivered with precision and care.

Steel Fabrication Image

Steel Fabrication

Our steel fabrication service covers the complete process from raw steel to finished structural components. We handle I-beams, channels, angles, hollow sections, and custom profiles for residential, commercial, and industrial applications.

Using CNC cutting, plasma cutting, and precision bending, we ensure every piece meets exact specifications and tolerances.

Structural Steel Custom Profiles CNC Cutting Plasma Cutting
Welding Services Image

Professional Welding

Our certified welders are skilled in all major welding processes — MIG, TIG, arc, and spot welding. We handle everything from light-gauge sheet metal to heavy structural steel, ensuring strong, clean, and durable welds.

All welding work is inspected and tested to meet the highest quality and safety standards.

MIG Welding TIG Welding Arc Welding Spot Welding

Gates, Grills & Railings

From grand main gates to intricate window grills and secure balcony railings, we design and fabricate metalwork that combines beauty with security. Our designs range from classic to contemporary.

All our gates and grills are powder-coated or painted for long-lasting, weather-resistant finish.

Main Gates Window Grills Balcony Railings Boundary Fencing

Metal Painting & Finishing

We offer professional powder coating, epoxy painting, anti-rust treatment, and hot-dip galvanizing services. Our finishing processes protect your metalwork from corrosion, UV damage, and wear for decades.

Available in a wide range of colors and finishes to match any aesthetic requirement.

Powder Coating Epoxy Paint Anti-Rust Treatment Galvanizing

Staircase Fabrication

We design and build stunning metal staircases — spiral, straight, helical, and floating designs — for both indoor and outdoor applications. Our staircases are engineered for safety and crafted for visual appeal.

Available in mild steel, stainless steel, and wrought iron to suit your design theme.
