Who We Are

At KFE, we are a Hyderabad-based engineering firm specializing in the design, fabrication, and installation of small-scale metal structures and process equipment. We provide complete, end-to-end solutions — from concept design and engineering analysis to manufacturing and on-site erection — ensuring precision, durability, and performance in every project.

Our company is led by Abdul Qadir, B.E. Mechanical Engineer and Managing Director, whose technical expertise and hands-on experience form the backbone of our operations. Under his leadership, we integrate sound engineering practices with modern fabrication techniques to deliver reliable, efficient, and cost-effective solutions tailored to diverse client requirements.

We serve residential, commercial, and industrial sectors, focusing on quality workmanship, safety, and timely project execution.
