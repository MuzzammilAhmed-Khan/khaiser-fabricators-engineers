Who We Are

Khaiser Fabricators and Engineers is a premier metal fabrication company based in Hyderabad, India. Founded in 2005, we have spent over two decades building a reputation for excellence, reliability, and craftsmanship in the metal fabrication industry.

From our state-of-the-art workshop equipped with modern machinery, we serve residential clients, commercial developers, and industrial businesses across India. Our team of certified engineers, expert welders, and skilled craftsmen brings unmatched expertise to every project we undertake.

We specialize in structural steel, custom gates and grills, staircases, metal sheds, and bespoke engineering fabrication — all delivered on time, within budget, and to the highest quality standards.

Over 1,500 successful projects completed across residential, commercial, and industrial sectors.

A team of 50+ skilled professionals including engineers, welders, and design specialists.

Modern CNC cutting, plasma cutting, and precision welding equipment for superior output.

Trusted by leading property developers, construction firms, and homeowners nationwide.

Our Mission & Vision

Mission: To deliver precision-engineered metal fabrication solutions that exceed our clients' expectations in quality, durability, and design — while maintaining honesty, safety, and value in every project.

Vision: To become the most trusted name in metal fabrication and engineering across India, recognized for innovation, craftsmanship, and an unwavering commitment to excellence.

We believe that every client deserves the best — whether it's a simple window grill or a complex industrial steel structure. Our commitment to quality never wavers, regardless of project size.
